For frame 3 (normalized \(t=0.5\)), the pendulum first touches the fixed rubber stop and has not yet rebounded. Use \(+x\) right, \(+y\) depth, \(+z\) up; the motion remains in the vertical plane \(y=0\). The fixed pivot is \(P=(0,0,0.62)\), and the bob radius is \(r=0.11\). In the first frame the bob center is \(C_0=(-0.58,0,0.10)\), so the pivot-to-center length is

\[
L=\sqrt{0.58^2+0.52^2}=\sqrt{0.6068}=0.778974.
\]

Thus the taut rod from the pivot to the bob surface has length \(L-r=0.668974\).

The rubber stop has a left vertical contact face at \(x=0.38\), with \(z\) between about \(-0.17\) and \(-0.05\), and depth \(y\in[-0.07,0.07]\). At first contact the bob center must be one radius to the left of that face:

\[
C_x=0.38-r=0.38-0.11=0.27.
\]

Enforcing the fixed pivot-to-center distance \(L\) gives

\[
(C_x)^2+(C_z-0.62)^2=L^2,
\]

so

\[
C_z=0.62-\sqrt{0.6068-0.27^2}
=0.62-\sqrt{0.5339}
=0.62-0.730685
=-0.110685.
\]

The contact point is therefore \((0.38,0,-0.110685)\), and the bob center is \(C=(0.27,0,-0.110685)\).

The rod endpoint is the sphere point on the pivot side, \(C-r u\), where \(u\) is the unit vector from the pivot to the bob center:

\[
u=\frac{(0.27,0,-0.110685-0.62)}{0.778974}
=\frac{(0.27,0,-0.730685)}{0.778974}
=(0.346609,0,-0.93801).
\]

Hence

\[
C-r u
=(0.27,0,-0.110685)-0.11(0.346609,0,-0.93801)
=(0.231873,0,-0.007504).
\]

So the rod is drawn from \((0,0,0.62)\) to \((0.231873,0,-0.007504)\).

The bob’s great-circle strokes rotate with the pendulum about the \(y\)-axis through its center. The initial center direction from the pivot is \((-0.58,-0.52)\), at angle \(-138.1^\circ\). The contact center direction is \((0.27,-0.730685)\), at angle \(-69.7^\circ\). The rotation increment is therefore \(\theta=68.4^\circ\), with

\[
\cos\theta
=\frac{(-0.58)(0.27)+(-0.52)(-0.730685)}{0.6068}
=0.36809,
\]

\[
\sin\theta
=\frac{(-0.58)(-0.730685)-(-0.52)(0.27)}{0.6068}
=0.92979.
\]

For a point with center-relative coordinates \((x_{\rm rel},y,z_{\rm rel})\), use the \(y\)-axis rotation

\[
x'_{\rm rel}=x_{\rm rel}\cos\theta-z_{\rm rel}\sin\theta,\qquad
z'_{\rm rel}=x_{\rm rel}\sin\theta+z_{\rm rel}\cos\theta,
\]

then add \(C=(0.27,0,-0.110685)\).

\[

=(0.85,0,-0.210685).
\]
