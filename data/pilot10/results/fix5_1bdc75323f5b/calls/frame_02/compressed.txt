Using \(+x\) right, \(+y\) depth, \(+z\) up, the initial cube has bottom-square vertices  
\(A=(0.209185,0.4486,-0.6)\),  
\(B=(0.4486,-0.209185,-0.6)\),  
\(C=(-0.209185,-0.4486,-0.6)\),  
\(D=(-0.4486,0.209185,-0.6)\).  
The top vertices have the same \(x,y\) at \(z=0.1\). The square is centered on the vertical axis \(x=y=0\), with radius  
\(\sqrt{0.209185^2+0.4486^2}\approx0.495\), so its side is about \(0.7\).

For frame 2, \(t=.25\), the cube yaws by \(22.5^\circ\) about the fixed vertical center axis. Choose positive yaw as counterclockwise viewed from \(+z\), so every vertex keeps its \(z\)-coordinate and obeys  
\[
x'=x\cos\theta-y\sin\theta,\qquad
y'=x\sin\theta+y\cos\theta,
\]
with  
\[
\theta=22.5^\circ,\quad \cos\theta=0.9238795325,\quad \sin\theta=0.3826834324.
\]

For vertex \(A\), the numerical substitution is  
\[
x_A'=0.209185(0.9238795325)-0.4486(0.3826834324)
=0.1932617395-0.1716717878
=0.0215899517,
\]
\[
y_A'=0.209185(0.3826834324)+0.4486(0.9238795325)
=0.080051633+0.414452358
=0.494503991.
\]
Thus  
\[
A'=(0.021589952,\;0.494503991,\;-0.6).
\]

Applying the same rotation to the other three corners gives  
\[
B'=(0.494503991,\;-0.021589952,\;-0.6),
\]
\[
C'=(-0.021589952,\;-0.494503991,\;-0.6),
\]
\[
D'=(-0.494503991,\;0.021589952,\;-0.6).
\]
The top vertices are the same \(A',B',C',D'\) with \(z=0.1\).

The bottom edges connect \(A'B'\), \(B'C'\), \(C'D'\), \(D'A'\); the top edges connect the corresponding top vertices; four vertical edges connect each bottom vertex to its top counterpart from \(z=-0.6\) to \(z=0.1\). The floor line remains fixed at  
\[
(-0.55,0.15,-0.6)\ \text{to}\ (0.55,0.15,-0.6).
