The cube is rigid and rotates only by yaw about the fixed vertical center axis through \(x=y=0\), whose midpoint is \(z=(-0.6+0.1)/2=-0.25\). The bottom face remains at \(z=-0.6\), the top face at \(z=0.1\), so every vertex keeps its \(z\)-coordinate. Frame 3 is at normalized time \(0.5\), an absolute yaw of \(45^\circ\) from the first frame; the previous frame 2 already has \(v_0\) at \(87.5^\circ\), i.e. \(+22.5^\circ\), so another \(+22.5^\circ\) gives frame 3.

From the first frame, the bottom square vertices in order are
\[
v_0=(0.209185,0.4486),\quad
v_1=(0.4486,-0.209185),\quad
v_2=(-0.209185,-0.4486),\quad
v_3=(-0.4486,0.209185)
\]
at \(z=-0.6\). The edge \(v_1-v_0=(0.239415,-0.657785)\) has length
\[
\sqrt{0.239415^2+(-0.657785)^2}=0.7,
\]
so the horizontal circumradius is \(r=0.7/\sqrt2=0.4949747468\). Equivalently, the initial polar angles are \(65^\circ,-25^\circ,-115^\circ,155^\circ\); adding \(45^\circ\) gives \(110^\circ,20^\circ,-70^\circ,200^\circ\).

Using the \(z\)-axis rotation formula for \(+45^\circ\),
\[
x'=\frac{x-y}{\sqrt2},\qquad
y'=\frac{x+y}{\sqrt2},
\]
for \(v_0\):
\[
x'=\frac{0.209185-0.4486}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{-0.239415}{1.41421356}
=-0.169291,
\]
\[
y'=\frac{0.209185+0.4486}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{0.657785}{1.41421356}
=0.465124.
\]
Thus \(v_0'=(-0.169291,0.465124)\). Applying the same rotation to the other vertices gives
\[
v_1'=(0.465124,0.169291),\quad
v_2'=(0.169291,-0.465124),\quad
v_3'=(-0.465124,-0.169291).
\]
The top vertices use these same \(xy\) coordinates at \(z=0.1\).

The twelve edges are the bottom square \(v_0'v_1'v_2'v_3'v_0'\) at \(z=-0.6\), the corresponding top square at \(z=0.1\), and the four vertical edges joining each bottom vertex to the top vertex with the same \(xy\). The fixed floor support line is unchanged:
\[
M(-0.55,0.15,-0.6)\;L(0.55,0.15,-0.6).
\]
