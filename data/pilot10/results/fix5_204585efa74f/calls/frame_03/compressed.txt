At \(t=0.5\) the car is treated as one rigid part transformed from the first frame, not as an increment from the previous frame. With \(+x\) right, \(+y\) depth and \(+z\) up, the ramp rises along \(+y\), so a positive pitch about \(+x\) raises the front. The ramp slope is
\[
m=\frac{-0.38-(-0.60)}{0.10-(-0.22)}=\frac{0.22}{0.32}=0.6875,
\]
so
\[
\theta=\tan^{-1}(0.6875)\approx 0.6023\ \mathrm{rad},\quad
c=\cos\theta=\frac{16}{\sqrt{377}}\approx0.82404192,\quad
s=\sin\theta=\frac{11}{\sqrt{377}}\approx0.56652882.
\]
The ramp surface is
\[
z=-0.60+m(y+0.22)=0.6875y-0.44875.
\]

In the first frame the wheel centers are at \(y=-0.30\) (front), \(y=-0.58\) (rear), \(z=-0.545\), with radius \(0.055\); the wheel-bottom contact points have local \(z=-0.60\). A rigid pitch about \(x\) plus translation is
\[
y'=cy-sz+T_y,\qquad z'=sy+cz+T_z.
\]
Choose \(T_y,T_z\) so all four wheel bottoms lie on the ramp and the wheelbase contact midpoint is centered at \(y=-0.06\). For local contact \(z=-0.60\), the front and rear base \(y\)-values before translation are
\[
c(-0.30)-s(-0.60)=0.09270472,
\]
\[
c(-0.58)-s(-0.60)=-0.13802702.
\]
Their midpoint is \(-0.02266115\), hence
\[
T_y=-0.06-(-0.02266115)=-0.03733885.
\]
The front contact then has
\[
y'=0.09270472-0.03733885=0.05536587,
\]
and the ramp there requires
\[
z'=0.6875(0.05536587)-0.44875=-0.41068597.
\]
The corresponding base \(z\)-value is
\[
s(-0.30)+c(-0.60)=-0.66438380,
\]
so
\[
T_z=-0.41068597-(-0.66438380)=0.25369783.
\]
The negative \(T_y\) is not backward travel; it compensates for the large positive pitch contribution to the wheel-bottom coordinates.

Since a wheel is a circle of radius \(0.055\) in a plane of constant \(x\), the pitch moves its center and leaves its radius; the cubic control offset remains \(0.030375\). The resulting wheel contacts are front \((y,z)=(0.05536587,-0.41068597)\) and rear \((-0.17536587,-0.56931404)\), both on the ramp.
